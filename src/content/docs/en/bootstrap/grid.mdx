The grid has twelve columns and five breakpoints. A row holds columns that share its width; each column says how many of the twelve it takes at each breakpoint. Content stacks on small screens and sits side by side on wide ones.

## When to use it

<div class="ds-do-dont">
<Rule kind="do" title="Use it">

- Design for the small screen first and add columns as the width grows.
- Keep the sum of the columns of a row at 12.
- Use the same gutters in the whole page.

</Rule>
<Rule kind="dont" title="Don't">

- Nest more than two levels of rows.
- Use fixed widths in pixels for columns.
- Use the grid to align a single control; use spacing.

</Rule>
</div>

## Anatomy

A **container** (`container`, `container-fluid`) holds **rows** (`row`) that hold **columns** (`col`, `col-md-6`…). The gap between columns is the **gutter**.

## Combinations

| Combination | Use it for |
|---|---|
| Columns × breakpoints | `col-12 col-md-6 col-lg-4`: one column on a phone, two on a tablet, three on a desktop. |
| Row × gutters | Keep the same gutters (`g-3`) in the whole page. |
| Container × width | `container` for pages, `container-fluid` for tools that use the whole screen. |

## What Didactika changes and why

| What we changed | Why it helps |
|---|---|
| The grid is untouched | Layout is the one thing every plugin shares with Bootstrap and with Moodle's own pages, so Didactika does not change it. |

## Classes

| Class | What it does |
|---|---|
| `container`, `container-fluid` | Fixed-width and full-width wrapper |
| `row` | A horizontal group of columns |
| `col`, `col-6`, `col-md-4` | A column: automatic, 6 of 12, 4 of 12 from the medium breakpoint |
| <Only of="v53" inline>`g-0`, `g-3`</Only><Only of="v45,v41" inline>`no-gutters`</Only> | Gutter between columns |

## In this version

</Only>

<Only of="v45">

## Accessibility

- The reading order is the order in the markup: do not rely on the grid to reorder what matters.
- Content must still make sense when it stacks.
- Never require horizontal scrolling at 320 px wide.
