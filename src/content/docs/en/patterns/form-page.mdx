A form page asks for data once and well. It is a card with a title, the fields in the order in which people think of them, and the actions at the foot.

## Rules

- **One column** for the fields that are filled in one after another; two columns only for pairs that belong together (first and last name, from and to).
- **Required fields** are marked with an asterisk and the rule is said once under the title.
- **Help under the field**, errors in its place and in text; never only a red border.
- **Group long forms in sections** with a title each, or in steps if the order matters.
- **The actions at the foot**: the main one on the right with the name of what it does ("Create course"), Cancel before it.
- **Validate when leaving the field** and again when sending; on sending, move the focus to the first error.

## Built with

Cards, text fields, selects, checks, validation messages and buttons. For a task with several stages, add the steps component and keep Back and Next at the foot.

## States

| State | What the user sees |
|---|---|
| Sending | The main button is disabled with a small spinner and the text "Saving…" |
| Saved | A toast with the result, or the page the user goes to next |
| Error | An alert at the top that lists the errors and links to each field, and the errors by their fields |
