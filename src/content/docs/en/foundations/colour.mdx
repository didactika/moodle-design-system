Plugins never write a colour. They use the classes and variables of Bootstrap and Moodle (`btn-primary`, `text-danger`, `--bs-primary`), and the theme of the site decides what they look like. Every swatch below is read from the stylesheet of the selected Moodle version, shows the best text colour on it with its contrast ratio, and copies its value when you click it.

### Didactika's palette

Switch on **Didactika colours** in the bar at the top and every example of the site, including the swatches below, is drawn with Didactika's palette applied to the Bootstrap roles: primary `#0f766e`, secondary `#475569`, success `#15803d`, info `#0e7490`, warning `#b45309` and danger `#b91c1c`. ## What each colour means

| Colour | Means | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| Primary | The main action, what is chosen or open | New profile, Save, the active page |
| Secondary | A neutral action or state | Cancel, Import, Draft |
| Success | Done, enabled, safe | Completed, Enabled |
| Warning | Needs attention, overwrites, partial | Partial, Overwrite |
| Danger | Removes, stops or failed | Delete, Stop, Failed |
| Info | Neutral information that matters | Queued, Running |

The meaning is the same in every plugin. Red is never decoration, and green is never "primary".

## Contrast

Text on a colour must reach 4.5:1 (3:1 from 24 px or 19 px bold), and the borders of controls and focus rings 3:1, in every colour mode. The badge on each swatch shows the best text colour and its ratio. Do not tell two states apart by hue alone: pair colour with an icon or a word, and use blue and orange before red and green.

## Tints

Where a plugin needs a soft background in a theme colour (a badge, a row action, a tile), it mixes the colour with the page background instead of choosing a new colour: `color-mix(in srgb, var(--bs-success) 14%, var(--bs-body-bg))`. The tint follows the theme and the colour mode, and nothing about it has to be configured.
