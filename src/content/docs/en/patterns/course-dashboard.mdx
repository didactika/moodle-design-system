A dashboard answers three questions in this order: how are things, what needs me, and where are we going. It does not replace the lists: every figure leads to the list behind it.

## The pieces

| Piece | Answers | Built with |
|---|---|---|
| Figures (2–4) | How are things | Cards with a tile, a value and a label; a trend only when it means something |
| A list of what needs attention | What needs me | A list group with a tile, a line and a badge that says why (days left, overdue) |
| Progress | Where are we going | Progress bars with the value beside them, colour by meaning |

## Rules

- No more than four figures; if there are more, they are in a table below.
- A figure that is bad news says so with colour and an icon, never with colour alone.
- The order of "what needs me" is the order of urgency, not alphabetical.
- Every card has an action or a link that leads to the detail; a dead figure is decoration.
- On a narrow screen the pieces stack in the same order.

## States

A new course with no data shows empty states in each card instead of zeroes: "No activity yet".
