A timeline tells the history of one thing: a course, a submission, an import. Each event is a dot on a line, with what happened and who did it and when.

## When to use it

<div class="ds-do-dont">
<Rule kind="do" title="Use it">

- Newest first, one line for what happened and one for who and when.
- Colour the dot only for what matters: a success, a warning or a failure.
- Link the title when the event has a page.

</Rule>
<Rule kind="dont" title="Don't">

- Use it for the steps of a task: those are steps.
- Write a paragraph per event.
- Colour every dot.

</Rule>
</div>

## Anatomy

An ordered list (`dd-timeline`) of **events** (`dd-timeline-item`) with a **title** (`dd-timeline-title`) and a **meta** line (`dd-timeline-meta`). A line joins the dots; `is-success`, `is-warning` and `is-danger` fill a dot.

## Combinations

| Combination | Use it for |
|---|---|
| Timeline × card | The history of an object in a side panel or below its details. |
| Timeline × avatars | The person who did it, beside the line of who and when. |
| Timeline × empty state | An object with no history yet. |

## What Didactika adds and why

| What it gives | Why it helps |
|---|---|
| A dot on a line | The order of events is read without reading the dates. |
| Colour only for what matters | The failure in a long history is found at once. |

## Classes

| Class | What it does |
|---|---|
| `dd-timeline` | The list |
| `dd-timeline-item` | An event; `is-success`, `is-warning`, `is-danger` |
| `dd-timeline-title`, `dd-timeline-meta` | What happened, and who and when |

## Accessibility

- An ordered list: the order is part of the meaning.
- Never rely on the colour of the dot: the title says what happened.
- Dates are written in full in the meta line or in a `time` element.
