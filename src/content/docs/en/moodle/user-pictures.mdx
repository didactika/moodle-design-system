Moodle shows a person with their photo or, when there is none, with their initials on a round background (`userinitials`). The design system keeps them as Moodle draws them and adds the stack for groups.

## Variants

<Example title="Initials, group and name" src="user-pictures/basic" />

## Sizes

| Class | Size | Use |
|---|---|---|
| `size-30` | 30 px | Lists and rows |
| `size-35` | 35 px | The user menu and comments |
| `size-50` | 50 px | A person beside their name and role |
| `size-64` | 64 px | A card of a person |
| `size-100` | 100 px | The head of a profile |

## Rules

- Always a `title` or the name next to it: the initials alone do not name anyone.
- A stack shows at most three people and a `+N`; the group has a label that lists the first names and the number of the rest.
- The photo is round, as the initials; never mix shapes in a row.

## Classes

| Class | What it does |
|---|---|
| `userinitials` | The round avatar with initials, written by Moodle |
| `size-30` … `size-100` | The size |
| `dd-avatars` | A stack of avatars that overlap (Didactika) |
| `dd-avatars-more` | The last one, with the number of people not shown (Didactika) |
