A **tooltip** is a one-line name or hint shown on hover and focus. A **popover** is a small card with a title and a few lines, opened on click. Neither holds anything the user needs in order to continue.

## When to use it

<div class="ds-do-dont">
<Rule kind="do" title="Use it">

- Tooltip: name an icon-only button, or give a short hint.
- Popover: explain a term or a setting in two or three lines.
- Open them on focus as well as on hover.

</Rule>
<Rule kind="dont" title="Don't">

- Put essential information, links or buttons in a tooltip.
- Use a popover for a form or a long text; use a modal or a page.
- Cover the element they describe.

</Rule>
</div>

## Anatomy

A tooltip is a box (`tooltip`) with an **arrow** and an **inner** text. A popover is a box (`popover`) with an **arrow**, a **header** and a **body**. Both point at their trigger from the side that has room.

## The tip of a title

A button with only an icon, a link that opens elsewhere or a badge that needs explaining carries a `title`. The browser draws that text in a grey box that cannot be styled, so the design system shows it in its own tip: a dark bubble with an arrow, a little rounder with the Didactika theme. It appears after a short wait under the pointer and at once with the keyboard, goes away on leaving, on a click, on Escape and on scrolling, and keeps an accessible name for the element. Hover or tab to the examples below.

The tip is for a short label (a few words). Anything longer, with a link or a button, is a popover.

## Combinations

| Combination | Use it for |
|---|---|
| Tooltip × icon button | Names a button that has only an icon; always together with its `aria-label`. |
| Popover × term | Explains a term or a setting in two or three lines, opened on click. |
| Placement | Top by default; the note flips to the other side when there is no room. |

## What Didactika changes and why

| What we changed | Why it helps |
|---|---|
| The tooltip is dark with light text | It is the only dark thing that floats, so it is never mistaken for content, and it reads over any background. |
| The popover is a card with a title and a quiet body | It is a small page of its own: it has a heading and room, not just a coloured box. |
| They flip when there is no room | The note is never cut off at the edge of the screen or of its container. |

## Accessibility

- The trigger is focusable and the tooltip opens on focus, and Escape closes it.
- A tooltip is linked with `aria-describedby`; do not use it as the only label of a control.
- A popover can be reached by keyboard and closes with Escape.
