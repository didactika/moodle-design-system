A modal interrupts on purpose: it asks for something the user must answer before going on, such as a confirmation or a short form. Keep it for that.

## When to use it

<div class="ds-do-dont">
<Rule kind="do" title="Use it">

- A clear title that says what is asked, and buttons named by their action ("Delete", not "OK").
- The main action on the right, cancel next to it; danger for what deletes.
- Keep it short; a long form deserves its own page.
- Close on Escape, on the close button and on Cancel.

</Rule>
<Rule kind="dont" title="Don't">

- Open a modal from another modal.
- Use it for information that does not need an answer; use an alert.
- Open one without the user asking for it.

</Rule>
</div>

## Anatomy

A **dialog** (`modal-dialog`) over a dimmed page, with a **header** (title and close), a **body** and a **footer** with the actions. Sizes: small, default, large and extra large.

## Variants and states

### Form

A title that says what is asked, the fields, and the actions at the foot: the main one on the right, cancel as a link beside it.

### Confirmation

For what deletes or cannot be undone. A tile with the danger icon, a title that is a question, and a button named by its action.

After something finished, a dialog that says so and offers the next step.

## Combinations

| Combination | Use it for |
|---|---|
| Size × content | Small (`modal-sm`) for a question, default for a form, large (`modal-lg`) for a list or a preview, extra large (`modal-xl`) for a table. |
| Icon tile × meaning | Danger for what deletes, success for a result, info for a notice; no tile for a plain form. |
| Scrollable × long content | For terms and lists; never for a long form, which is a page. |
| Steps × flow | A progress bar of steps, and Back / Cancel / Next in the foot; no more than three steps. |
| Centred × short | Vertically centred (`modal-dialog-centered`) for a short question; at the top for a form that may grow. |

## What Didactika changes and why

| What we changed | Why it helps |
|---|---|
| Large radius, a soft deep shadow, no lines between header, body and foot | The dialog is one surface that floats; lines would split it into three boxes. |
| Cancel is a link and the main action is filled | There is one thing to press; leaving is easy but never competes with it. |
| The title is a question or a verb and the button repeats it | "Delete course?" / "Delete": the user knows what will happen without reading the body. |
| The icon tile sets the tone before the text | A red tile asks for care before a word is read. |

## Accessibility

- `role="dialog"` (or `alertdialog` for a confirmation) with `aria-labelledby` pointing at the title.
- Focus moves into the modal when it opens, stays inside while open, and returns to the trigger when it closes.
- Escape closes it; the page behind is not reachable.
