A segmented control switches between views of the same content, without leaving the page. It is a group of buttons on a track, with the chosen one raised.

## When to use it

<div class="ds-do-dont">
<Rule kind="do" title="Use it">

- Two to four options with short labels (and an icon if it helps).
- To change how the same data is shown: grid or list, day or week.
- `aria-pressed` on each button and a label on the group.

</Rule>
<Rule kind="dont" title="Don't">

- Use it to move between pages: those are tabs.
- Use it for a setting that applies on saving: use radio buttons.
- Put more than four options in it.

</Rule>
</div>

## Anatomy

A track (`dd-segmented`) that holds the **buttons**. The chosen one has `aria-pressed="true"` and is raised over the track.

## Combinations

| Combination | Use it for |
|---|---|
| Segmented × list | Cards or list: the same items, another shape. |
| Segmented × chart | Day, week or month: the same series, another range. |
| With icons | When the options are views and the icon says it faster than the word. |

## What Didactika adds and why

| What it gives | Why it helps |
|---|---|
| A raised chosen option on a track | It reads as one control with one selection, unlike a row of separate buttons. |
| Immediate effect | The content changes on pressing, so there is no "Apply". |

## Classes

| Class | What it does |
|---|---|
| `dd-segmented` | The track with its buttons |
| `aria-pressed="true"` or `active` | The chosen button |

## Accessibility

- A `group` with a label; each button states its pressed state with `aria-pressed`.
- Arrows may move between the options; Enter and Space choose.
- A disabled option is dimmed and skipped.
