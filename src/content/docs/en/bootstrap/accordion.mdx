An accordion stacks sections of which one is open at a time; collapse shows or hides one block. Use them for content that most people do not need all at once.

## When to use it

<div class="ds-do-dont">
<Rule kind="do" title="Use it">

- Short, specific headings: they are what people scan.
- Open the section most people need first.
- Use collapse for optional details ("Show more").

</Rule>
<Rule kind="dont" title="Don't">

- Hide content the user needs to finish the task.
- Nest accordions.
- Use an accordion where tabs would show the views side by side.

</Rule>
</div>

## Anatomy

A group (`accordion`) of **items** (`accordion-item`), each with a **header button** (`accordion-button`) and a **panel** (`accordion-collapse` with `accordion-body`). A plain **collapse** is a button that shows or hides a `collapse` block.

## Accessibility

- The header is a `button` with `aria-expanded` and `aria-controls`.
- Enter and Space open and close; Tab moves between headers.
- The heading level fits the page: wrap the button in `h2`/`h3`.
