A progress bar shows a known amount of completion. When the amount is not known (waiting for a server), use a spinner instead.

## When to use it

<div class="ds-do-dont">
<Rule kind="do" title="Use it">

- Show the number next to the bar when the exact value matters.
- Use the success colour only for completion.
- Give it a text label that says what progresses.

</Rule>
<Rule kind="dont" title="Don't">

- Use a bar when you cannot tell how long it will take.
- Animate a bar that is not changing.
- Use it as a chart.

</Rule>
</div>

## Anatomy

A **track** (`progress`) and one or more **bars** (`progress-bar`) whose width is the value. A bar can be striped, animated and carry a label.

## Combinations

| Combination | Use it for |
|---|---|
| Single bar × value | One bar, the number at the side or above ("64%", "4 of 6 activities"). |
| Stacked × total | Parts of one total in one bar, joined, with a legend of dots. |
| Steps × flow | One small bar per step, the finished ones filled; with "Step 3 of 5" above. |
| Colour × meaning | Primary for progress, success for completion, warning and danger when a limit is close (storage). |
| Striped × waiting | Striped and animated only while something is really working. |

## What Didactika changes and why

| What we changed | Why it helps |
|---|---|
| The bars of one total join with no gap | Gaps look like separate quantities; a joined bar shows one total split in parts. |
| The ends of the track are round, the joints are straight | The bar reads as one object and the parts are told by colour, not by shape. |
| The number is always written | Progress must not depend on guessing a width: people with low vision or colour blindness read the number. |
| Steps are separate small bars | A person counting steps counts blocks; one long bar hides how many remain. |

## Classes

| Class | What it does |
|---|---|
| `progress` | The track; its height sets the bar height |
| `progress-bar` | The filled part; width in `style` |
| `progress-bar-striped`, `progress-bar-animated` | Stripes and movement |
| `bg-success`… | Colour of a bar |
| `dd-progress`, `dd-progress-head`, `dd-progress-value` | A progress bar with its title and value (Didactika) |
| `dd-progress-inline` | The value at the side of the bar (Didactika) |
| `dd-progress-steps` | One small bar per step (Didactika) |

## Accessibility

- `role="progressbar"` with `aria-valuenow`, `aria-valuemin`, `aria-valuemax` and an `aria-label`.
- The value is also written as text when it matters.
- Animated stripes respect the reduced motion preference.
