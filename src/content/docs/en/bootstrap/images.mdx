Images should never break the layout: they scale with their container and keep their proportions. A figure ties an image to its caption.

## When to use it

<div class="ds-do-dont">
<Rule kind="do" title="Use it">

- Make images fluid (`img-fluid`) so they never overflow.
- Give every meaningful image alternative text; mark decorative ones with an empty `alt`.
- Use a figure with a caption when the image needs explaining.

</Rule>
<Rule kind="dont" title="Don't">

- Put text inside an image.
- Stretch an image: crop it instead.
- Use an image for something a real icon can say.

</Rule>
</div>

## Anatomy

An image (`img`) with a shape (`rounded`, `rounded-circle`, `img-thumbnail`), alone or inside a **figure** (`figure`) with a **caption** (`figure-caption`).

## Combinations

| Combination | Use it for |
|---|---|
| Fluid × thumbnail | `img-fluid` for every image; the thumbnail for a small preview with a frame. |
| Rounded × circle | Rounded for photos, a circle only for people. |
| Figure × caption | A figure when the image needs a sentence to be understood. |

## What Didactika changes and why

| What we changed | Why it helps |
|---|---|
| The thumbnail frame is a hairline with a radius | It matches the borders of cards and fields instead of a heavy grey line. |
| The caption is small and muted | It explains the image without competing with the text around it. |

## Classes

| Class | What it does |
|---|---|
| `img-fluid` | Scales down with its container |
| `img-thumbnail` | Border and padding |
| `rounded`, `rounded-circle` | Shape |
| `figure`, `figure-img`, `figure-caption` | Image with caption |

## Accessibility

- Meaningful images have `alt` text that says what they show, not that they are images.
- Decorative images have `alt=""`.
- A caption is real text, not part of the image.
