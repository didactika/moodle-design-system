A task with several stages (an import, a course set-up) shows where the user is and what is left. Steps are the indicator, not the navigation: they say "step 2 of 4"; the buttons of the screen move between them.

## When to use it

<div class="ds-do-dont">
<Rule kind="do" title="Use it">

- Between three and six steps, each with a short label.
- Mark the current one with `aria-current="step"` and the done ones with `is-done`.
- Put Back and Next at the foot of the screen, not on the steps.

</Rule>
<Rule kind="dont" title="Don't">

- Use steps for a task with a single stage.
- Hide the labels on a wide screen: the number alone does not say where you are.
- Make the steps a menu that skips ahead if the earlier ones are not valid.

</Rule>
</div>

## Anatomy

An ordered list (`dd-steps`) whose items (`dd-step`) take a number from a counter, a **label** (`dd-step-label`) and a **line** that joins each one to the next. A step is `is-done` (check and filled line), `is-current` (ringed in the primary colour) or neither (quiet).

## Variants and states

Horizontal for a wizard in a page or a dialog; vertical (`dd-steps-vertical`) when the labels are long or the space is narrow.

## Combinations

| Combination | Use it for |
|---|---|
| Steps × dialog | The wizard of an import: steps at the top, the choices in the body, Back and Next at the foot. |
| Steps × progress bar | Steps for the stages, a progress bar inside the current one if it takes long. |
| Vertical × side panel | A set-up checklist beside a form. |

## What Didactika adds and why

| What it gives | Why it helps |
|---|---|
| The number comes from a counter, the check from the state | The order cannot be written wrong in the markup, and a done step always looks done. |
| The line fills as the task advances | The user sees how much is left at a glance, without reading. |
| The current step has a ring, not only a colour | It stays visible for users who do not tell colours apart. |

## Classes

| Class | What it does |
|---|---|
| `dd-steps` | The list |
| `dd-step` | One step |
| `dd-step-label` | Its label |
| `is-done` | A step that is finished |
| `is-current` | The step the user is on |
| `dd-steps-vertical` | Steps in a column |

## Accessibility

- An `ol` with an `aria-label` that names the task.
- `aria-current="step"` on the current step.
- The state is also in the text for assistive technology ("completed"), not only in the mark.
