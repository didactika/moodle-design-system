A floating label saves vertical space: the label is the placeholder until the field has a value, and then it shrinks above it. Prefer a normal label in Moodle forms; use this one for short, stand-alone forms such as a sign-in or a quick filter.

## When to use it

<div class="ds-do-dont">
<Rule kind="do" title="Use it">

- Short labels that fit inside the field.
- The same `id` and `for` link as any label.
- A placeholder (even a space) in the field: the effect depends on it.

</Rule>
<Rule kind="dont" title="Don't">

- Use it in long forms or next to fields with a normal label.
- Use long labels; they are cut.
- Use it with a field that has an add-on in an input group.

</Rule>
</div>

## Anatomy

A floating field has the **control** and the **label** after it, both inside `form-floating`. The label is drawn over the control and moves when the control has focus or a value.

## Combinations

| Combination | Use it for |
|---|---|
| Floating × short form | A sign-in, a quick filter, a short form with 1 to 4 fields. |
| Floating × select | The select needs `w-100` so it fills the box; the label is always raised. |
| Floating × validation | `is-invalid` and its message work as on any field. |

## What Didactika changes and why

| What we changed | Why it helps |
|---|---|
| The label keeps the size and colour of a placeholder until it rises | The form is compact, but the label is never lost: it shrinks above the value instead of disappearing. |
| Same side padding as the other fields | A floating field and a normal one line up when they share a page. |

## Classes

| Class | What it does |
|---|---|
| `form-floating` | Wraps the control and its label (the label comes after the control) |

## In this version

<Only of="v53">

Bootstrap 5.3 draws the focus as a soft ring and keeps every colour in CSS variables (`--bs-*`), so a theme recolours a control with one variable.

</Only>

## Accessibility

- The label stays linked with `for`: a screen reader reads it as usual.
- Contrast of the shrunken label is the same 4.5:1.
- The placeholder is not the label.
